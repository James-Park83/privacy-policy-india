Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect and use your data in accordance with India's DPDP Act.

1. Specific Information We Collect

  • Photos and Media: We access your Photo Gallery to allow you to upload material design previews and project-related images for data transfer.
  • Field Notes & Daily Wage: We collect work logs, site categories, and titles to calculate labor costs and manage field expenses.
  • GPS & Location Data: We collect precise location information to provide regional job listings and manage site-specific field notes.
  • Contact Information (Phone): For our Job Recruitment services, we collect your Mobile Phone Number to enable direct communication between employers and job seekers.
  • Technical Estimation Data: Dimensions (Width, Height) and material specifications (Stainless, Glass, Aluminum, etc.) for accurate cost calculation.

2. Purpose of Data Processing

  • To generate professional PDF quotes and design previews.
  • To connect skilled laborers with employers via direct contact.
  • To track field work progress and manage labor costs (Man-day) effectively.

3. Data Deletion & Grievance

You can request data deletion by contacting us. We provide an in-app feature to delete job postings and estimates.
